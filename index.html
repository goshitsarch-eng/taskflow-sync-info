TaskFlow

A personal desktop task manager that saves your edits locally, with optional Google Drive sync between your computers.

Your tasks, on your computers

Organize tasks with projects, sections, labels, descriptions, comments, reminders, and attachments. Local editing remains available offline.

Optional Google Drive sync

Sign in from TaskFlow to connect one Google account to your personal workspace. TaskFlow uses your account identity and email to identify the workspace, and its private application storage in Drive to synchronize shared content, preferences, attachments, and recovery history.

This permission does not provide access to ordinary Drive files. Each computer signs in separately; TaskFlow does not operate a separate cloud server.

Read the privacy policy for data handling, retention, and disconnect behavior.
